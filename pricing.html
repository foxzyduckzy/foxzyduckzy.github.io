Pricing questions

Yes. The Free plan is a permanent tier, not a trial — no card, no expiry. Your $1 weekly credit refreshes every Monday.

Every plan includes a weekly credit allowance that resets every Monday. Credit pays for AI usage as you go, priced by the model you pick. Unused weekly credit does not carry over — but top-up credit you buy never expires and is spent only after your weekly allowance runs out.

Inside the app: download Loopable8, sign in, and open Upgrade plan from your account menu. Checkout is handled by Paddle, our payment provider — your card details are entered on Paddle's secure page, never in the app.

Prices are set in USD and Paddle presents them in your local currency at checkout, taxes included where applicable. Nothing is hidden — the total you approve is the total you pay.

Cancel anytime — monthly or yearly. You keep your plan until the end of the billing period, then drop to Free without losing any local data. Refunds are available within 14 days of first purchase; see the refund policy for the details.
