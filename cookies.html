Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

The short version: loopable8.com sets no tracking cookies — no advertising pixels, no cross-site identifiers, no third-party analytics. We use browser local storage for a handful of essential preferences, listed in full below.

1. What we store

KeyPurposeLifetime
lo8_themeRemembers your dark/light mode choiceUntil cleared
lo8_cookie_consentRemembers your consent banner choiceUntil cleared
lo8_user / lo8_sessionYour account and sign-in session (demo accounts are stored only in your browser)Until sign-out / deletion
lo8_newsletterNewsletter subscription statusUntil unsubscribed

2. What we don't use

  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel)
  • Fingerprinting or cross-site tracking of any kind

3. Third-party requests

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Font requests expose your IP address to Google as any web request does; Google states these requests are not used for tracking. If we self-host fonts at launch, this section will be updated.

4. Managing storage

Clear your browser's site data for loopable8.com to remove everything at once, or use your browser's developer tools to remove individual keys. The consent banner reappears after clearing.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@loopable8.com.
