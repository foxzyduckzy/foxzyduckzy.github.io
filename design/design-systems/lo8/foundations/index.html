Foundations

Space, form, motion

Depth is hairline borders and layered background tints, not drop shadows. Motion confirms a state change; it never decorates.

Depth

correct
Hairline border, layered tint
banned
Heavy default shadow

Flat surfaces separated by a 1px border, sitting on a background one step up or down the ramp. That is the entire depth system. It works because the ramp is ordered — the layering is already in the colour.

Motion

150–250ms

House curve: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Everything on this page stops moving entirely under prefers-reduced-motion, including the mark’s boil — which is the reason the boil is a CSS animation rather than SMIL.
