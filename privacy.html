Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026 · Applies to the Loopable8 application and loopable8.com

The short version: your conversations, memory and settings live on your machine. When you use AI features, only the context needed for that request is sent to the model API over an encrypted connection. We don't sell data, we don't train models on your code, and the app contains no third-party analytics SDK.

1. Who we are

Loopable8 Studio ("we", "us") builds the Loopable8 application and operates loopable8.com. For privacy questions, contact privacy@loopable8.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The application stores locally, under your user profile:

  • Conversation history (if "Save Conversations" is on)
  • Assistant memory (if "Save Memory" is on)
  • Run settings, workspace preferences and UI state
  • Logs for troubleshooting (rotated automatically)

You can clear memory from run settings at any time. Uninstalling removes all local application data.

3. Data sent to servers

When you invoke an AI feature, we transmit over TLS: the prompt, relevant code/file context you've opened or referenced, your run settings, and — if enabled — search grounding queries. This is processed to generate your response and is not used to train models.

If you create a website account, we store your name, email and plan to operate the service.

4. What we never do

  • Sell or rent personal data — to anyone, ever
  • Train models on your code or conversations
  • Embed advertising or cross-site tracking
  • Read files outside workspaces you open

5. Website storage

loopable8.com uses browser local storage for your theme preference, session and newsletter status. No tracking cookies are set. Details in the Cookie Policy.

6. Data retention & deletion

Local data persists until you delete it. Account data is removed within 30 days of account deletion. Newsletter subscriptions end immediately on unsubscribe.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR and similar laws), you may request access, correction, export or deletion of personal data we hold. Email privacy@loopable8.com — we respond within 30 days.

8. Children

Loopable8 is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We'll post updates here and, for material changes, notify you in-app or by email. The "last updated" date always reflects the current version.
