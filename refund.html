Refund Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

This policy explains when and how you can get your money back for a Loopable8 purchase. It sits alongside our Terms & Conditions; where the two mention refunds, they mean the same thing.

1. The short version

You can request a full refund within 14 days of your first purchase of a paid plan — no questions asked. After that window, subscriptions renew automatically and are non-refundable for the period already started, but you can cancel anytime to stop future charges.

2. Who you're paying

Payments for Loopable8 are processed by Paddle, our Merchant of Record. That means Paddle is the seller of record on your receipt and statement, handles billing and applicable taxes, and processes approved refunds back to your original payment method. Loopable8 never sees or stores your card details.

3. Subscriptions (Pro, Creator, Teams)

  • First purchase: a full refund is available within 14 days of your first payment for a plan.
  • Renewals: monthly and yearly plans renew automatically. Renewal charges are not automatically refundable, so cancel before the renewal date if you don't want to continue — you keep your plan until the end of the paid period, then drop to Free.
  • Yearly plans: the same 14-day window applies from the date of the annual charge. Outside it, we don't pro-rate unused months, but you keep full access until the year ends.

4. Credit top-ups

Top-up credit is a one-off purchase that adds to your balance and never expires.

  • Unused top-up can be refunded within 14 days of purchase.
  • Credit you've already spent on AI usage cannot be refunded, since the underlying cost has already been incurred.

Your weekly plan allowance is included with your subscription and is not separately refundable — it is covered by the subscription refund terms above.

5. How to request a refund

Email support@loopable8.com from the address on your account, with your order or receipt reference (Paddle emails you one after each purchase). We aim to reply within 2 business days. You can also request a refund directly from the receipt Paddle sent you. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

6. Free plan

The Free plan costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund. You can use it for as long as you like without ever entering a payment method.

7. Chargebacks

If something looks wrong on your statement, please contact us first — most issues are resolved faster than a bank dispute. Opening a chargeback without contacting us may result in the associated account being suspended while the dispute is reviewed.

8. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any refund or cancellation rights you have under the consumer law that applies to you. Where local law grants a longer or stronger right than the 14-day window above, that law prevails.

9. Contact

Questions about a charge or a refund? Reach us at support@loopable8.com or through the contact page.
